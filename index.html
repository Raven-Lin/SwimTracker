How the numbers are worked out

World Aquatics points (still widely called FINA points) score every swim against the world record for that gender, course and event: points = 1000 × (world record ÷ your time)³. The base time is worth 1000. It is the only fair way to compare a 12 y/o's 50 Free with a 17 y/o's 400 IM, which is exactly what you need across a 150-swimmer squad.

Personal best is the fastest time ever recorded in that exact course, distance and stroke. A long-course 100 Free and a short-course 100 Free are separate PBs, because they are separate events.

Season best is the fastest time in the current season, grouped by the season start month set above.

Form is the gap between the two. Coaches watch this more than any single time: a season best closing in on the personal best means the swimmer is coming into form.

Base times follow the published World Aquatics table. Records move, so the numbers live in one editable table in src/points.js. Changing a base time rescales everyone in that event by the same factor, so rankings within an event never shift.
